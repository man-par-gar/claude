Adaptive Robotic Welding Cell for Pressure Vessels

Designed a fully adaptive welding cell using a 6-axis FANUC arm with laser seam tracking and real-time parameter adjustment. The system handles variable joint fit-up on ASME-code pressure vessels, eliminating manual re-teaching and cutting setup time from 4 hours to under 20 minutes.

Robotics 2023

Multi-Robot Collaborative Assembly Line

Coordinated a 4-robot cell (2 ABB IRB 6700, 2 collaborative UR10e) for tack welding and material handling of structural steel frames. Implemented collision-free motion planning using ROS 2 MoveIt and achieved a 28% throughput gain over the previous manual process.

Welding 2022

Titanium Aerospace Bracket — WPS Development

Developed weld procedure specifications for Grade 5 titanium brackets used in aircraft secondary structures. Qualified procedures per AWS D17.1, including inert-gas purging protocols that achieved zero porosity on 100% of radiographic inspections.

Robotics 2021

Custom End-Effector for Thin-Wall Tube Welding

Designed and fabricated a dual-torch end-effector for orbital welding of thin-wall stainless steel tubing on a KUKA KR 10 R1100. Integrated vision-based joint detection and proprietary torch angle compensation logic to maintain consistent fusion on ±0.3 mm fit-up variation.
